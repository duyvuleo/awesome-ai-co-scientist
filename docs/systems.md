<a id="agentic-scientist-system"></a>

### [Agentic Scientist](https://github.com/SakanaAI/AI-Scientist-v3) · [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18933)

**2026-10-13 · preprint** · End-to-end research · code-available, coding-agents, evidence, machine-learning

Orchestrates coding agents through investigation, ablations, sealed evaluation, analysis, and manuscript production. The public implementation includes task snapshots, resumable runs, and an inspection interface. It is useful for studying research orchestration and evidence collection; successful execution and scientific validity still depend on the task, evaluator, and underlying agent.

Related entries: [The Agentic Scientist: Orchestrating Coding Agents for Automated Scientific (Co-)Discovery](papers.md#agentic-scientist).

Sources: [1](https://github.com/SakanaAI/AI-Scientist-v3) · [2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.18933) · Content checked: 2026-10-22.

### [ScientistTwo](https://scientist-two.github.io/) · [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19644)

**2026-09-17 · preprint** · End-to-end research · generated-artifacts, framework-code-unconfirmed, machine-learning, integrity-audit

Coordinates idea generation, experimental evaluation, ablation analysis, manuscript writing, and internal review in a closed research loop. The project releases generated research artifacts and reports broad benchmark comparisons plus integrity audits. Those results remain author-reported, and the public project page does not establish availability of the complete framework source.

Related entries: [ScientistTwo: Pioneering the Human Knowledge Frontier with Autonomous AI](papers.md#scientist-two).

Sources: [1](https://scientist-two.github.io/) · [2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19644) · Content checked: 2026-10-22.

<a id="sciencebuddy-system"></a>

### [ScienceBuddy](https://github.com/Gen-Verse/ScienceBuddy) · [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17523)

**2026-09-15 · preprint** · End-to-end research · research-code, hosted-preview, human-ai-collaboration, self-improvement

Combines an interactive scientific workspace with research on alternating harness improvement and model reinforcement learning. The public repository distinguishes its hosted workspace demonstration from open experiments using frozen tasks and simulated feedback. It is especially relevant to human feedback and persistent research context; deployed continual improvement is not established by the released experiment alone.

Related entries: [ScienceBuddy: Recursive-in-Recursive Self-Improvement for Interactive Scientific Agents](papers.md#sciencebuddy).

Sources: [1](https://github.com/Gen-Verse/ScienceBuddy) · [2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17523) · [3](https://phai-labs.com/en/papers/sciencebuddy/) · Content checked: 2026-10-22.

### [ScientistOne](https://scientist-one.github.io/) · [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.26340)

**2026-05-25 · preprint** · End-to-end research · generated-artifacts, framework-code-unconfirmed, provenance, integrity-audit

Connects literature investigation, algorithm discovery, writing, and claim verification through explicit evidence chains. #### [OpenResearch](https://github.com/alphaXiv/OpenResearch)

**2026 · project** · End-to-end research · code-available, local-workspace, experiment-tracking, compute

A local research workspace and harness from alphaXiv that connects coding agents to literature discovery, experiment execution, and artifact inspection. It records experiments in a local database and supports multiple compute backends. The repository documents a usable product; these capabilities are project claims rather than an independently established discovery benchmark.

## 2025

<a id="kosmos-system"></a>

### [Kosmos](https://edisonscientific.com/news/announcing-kosmos) · [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.02824)

**2025-11-04 · preprint** · End-to-end research · hosted, long-horizon, world-model, data-analysis

Combines literature-search and data-analysis agents with a structured world model that preserves information across long investigations. Edison provides the system through a hosted platform and reports discovery case studies with traceable supporting evidence. Reported productivity equivalents come from user estimates and modeling assumptions, rather than a controlled general measure of researcher replacement.

Related entries: [Kosmos: An AI Scientist for Autonomous Discovery](papers.md#kosmos).

Sources: [1](https://edisonscientific.com/news/announcing-kosmos) · [2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.02824) · Content checked: 2026-10-22.

<a id="ai-scientist-v2-system"></a>

### [The AI Scientist-v2](https://github.com/SakanaAI/AI-Scientist-v2) · [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.08066)

**2025-04-10 · peer-reviewed** · End-to-end research · code-available, tree-search, machine-learning, paper-generation

Uses an experiment manager and progressive tree search to explore research implementations without fixed starting templates. It integrates experiments, figures, manuscript writing, and review. The later Nature evaluation describes human selection of workshop submissions and withdrawal after review; workshop scores therefore should not be interpreted as an entirely unselected autonomous publication pipeline.

Related entries: [The AI Scientist-v2: Workshop-Level Automated Scientific Discovery via Agentic Tree Search](papers.md#ai-scientist-v2), [Towards end-to-end automation of AI research](papers.md#ai-scientist-nature).

Sources: [1](https://github.com/SakanaAI/AI-Scientist-v2) · [2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.08066) · [3](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-10265-5) · Content checked: 2026-10-22.

### [Google Co-Scientist](https://deepmind.google/blog/co-scientist-a-multi-agent-ai-partner-to-accelerate-research/) · [Paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-10644-y)

**2025-02-19 · peer-reviewed** · Ideation and planning · hosted, hypothesis-generation, multi-agent, human-ai-collaboration

A Gemini-based system in which agents generate, critique, rank, and refine scientific hypotheses in collaboration with researchers. Google describes biological case studies and an experimental Hypothesis Generation access route. This is a hosted research partner focused on hypothesis development; a general open-source implementation of Google's system was not confirmed.

Related entries: [Accelerating scientific discovery with Co-Scientist](papers.md#google-co-scientist).

<a id="virtual-lab-system"></a>

#### [Virtual Lab](https://github.com/zou-group/virtual-lab) · [Paper](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-09442-9)

**2025 · peer-reviewed** · End-to-end research · code-available, human-ai-collaboration, biomedicine, wet-lab-validation

Structures collaboration between a human researcher and specialist language-model agents through team and individual meetings. The public code includes the nanobody-design application accompanying a Nature study with experimental validation. This supplies a grounded example of collaborative scientific discovery, while the biological evidence applies to that specific campaign rather than unrestricted autonomous laboratory work.

Related entries: [The Virtual Lab of AI agents designs new SARS-CoV-2 nanobodies](papers.md#virtual-lab).

Sources: [1](https://github.com/zou-group/virtual-lab) · [2](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-025-09442-9) · Content checked: 2026-10-22.

#### [The AI Scientist](https://github.com/SakanaAI/AI-Scientist) · [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.06292)

**2024 · peer-reviewed** · End-to-end research · code-available, machine-learning, paper-generation, automated-review

Implements a template-based loop for proposing ideas, changing experimental code, running machine-learning experiments, and drafting papers with an automated reviewer. The repository provides executable workflows and example outputs. Its structured starting templates make it a useful baseline for research automation, while generated manuscripts still require inspection of results, citations, and methodological claims.

Related entries: [The AI Scientist: Towards Fully Automated Open-Ended Scientific Discovery](papers.md#ai-scientist), [Towards end-to-end automation of AI research](papers.md#ai-scientist-nature).
